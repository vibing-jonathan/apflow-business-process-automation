The project should be related to business process automation.
The general direction is this: create a tool that helps a company automate part of its daily operations. It could be something related to admin work, customer communication, reporting, task management, document processing, CRM updates, invoice handling, internal workflows, or any repetitive process that currently takes too much manual work.
You should choose one specific business problem and build an MVP that solves it in a practical way. It doesn’t have to be a huge platform. It can be a focused tool that automates one clear workflow well.
For example, you can think about questions like:
What repetitive task do companies often waste time on?
What kind of information does the user need to provide?
What should the system automate, generate, organize, or track?
Where could AI be useful in the process?
What would the workflow look like before and after automation?
What would make this project valuable from both a technical and business perspective?
The important part is that you don’t just build a random app. You should first define a real business problem, understand the manual process, design a simple automated solution, and then build an MVP around that solution.
The goal of the project is to show that you can identify a real business process that can be improved, think about it like a product, define your own solution, and build a practical technical project that demonstrates frontend, backend, database, workflow automation, and AI integration skills
